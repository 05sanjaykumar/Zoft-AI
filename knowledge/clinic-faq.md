# Bright Smile Dental: clinic FAQ

This is the mock clinic's knowledge base (`"knowledgeBase": "clinic-faq"`). The agent should
look it up during a call, not hold it in the prompt.

## Opening hours
Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Closed for lunch from 1:00 PM to 2:00 PM.
Closed on Sundays and public holidays. Appointments are 30 minutes.

## Address and parking
12 Lake View Road, T. Nagar, Chennai 600017, first floor above the pharmacy.
Free parking for patients in the basement: show your appointment at the gate.
Two-wheeler parking is in front of the building.

## Prices
- Consultation and check-up: ₹500
- Cleaning (scaling and polishing): ₹1,200
- Tooth-coloured filling: ₹1,500 per tooth
- Root canal treatment: ₹6,000 to ₹9,000 depending on the tooth
- Extraction: ₹1,000 (simple), ₹3,500 (surgical)
- Teeth whitening: ₹8,000

## Insurance and payment
We accept cashless claims from Star Health, HDFC ERGO and ICICI Lombard for covered treatments.
Bring your insurance card and a photo ID. Cash, UPI and all major cards are accepted.

## Cancellations
Please cancel or reschedule at least 4 hours before your appointment by calling the clinic.

## Emergencies
For severe pain, swelling or a knocked-out tooth during opening hours, call and we will see you
the same day. Outside opening hours, go to the nearest hospital emergency department.
